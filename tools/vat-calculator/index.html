This calculator helps UK users quickly add or remove VAT from any amount. It defaults to the standard 20% rate, but you can change it to match reduced or special rates.

How it works

Choose whether your figure already includes VAT or not, enter the amount and rate, and the tool works out the net, VAT, and gross total. Useful for quoting clients, checking invoices, or preparing bookkeeping figures.

Worked example

Amount excludes VAT: £100 net at 20% VAT → £20 VAT → £120 total.

Amount includes VAT: £120 gross at 20% VAT → net = £120 ÷ 1.20 = £100, VAT = £20.

Common questions

What is the standard UK VAT rate?

The standard rate is 20% for most goods and services. Some items are reduced-rated at 5%, and some are zero-rated or exempt.

How do I remove VAT from a total?

Divide the gross amount by 1 + (rate ÷ 100). For 20% VAT, divide by 1.20 to get the net amount.
