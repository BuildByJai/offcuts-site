When the box turns green, click or tap it as fast as you can. After a random delay, the tool measures the gap between the stimulus and your click, in milliseconds.

How it works

Click Start and wait. After a random delay between 1.5 and 4.5 seconds, the box turns green. Click before it turns green and it's a false start — it doesn't count.

Common questions

What is a good reaction time?

Around 250ms is average for a visual stimulus. Under 200ms is fast; competitive gamers often hit 150–200ms.
