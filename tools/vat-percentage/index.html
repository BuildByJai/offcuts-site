VAT Percentage UKfinance

Current UK VAT rates explained. Find the implied VAT rate from your figures.

The standard UK VAT rate is 20% for most goods and services. There are also reduced rates (5%, e.g. domestic energy), zero-rated supplies (e.g. most food, children's clothes), and exempt supplies (e.g. insurance, education).

Implied VAT rate

0%

How it works

The calculator divides the VAT amount by the net amount and multiplies by 100 to give you the implied VAT rate as a percentage. For example, net £100 and VAT £20 gives (20 ÷ 100) × 100 = 20%.

Common questions

What is the standard VAT percentage in the UK?

20%, for most goods and services.

Can these rates change and should I check HMRC?

Yes — VAT rates and category treatment can change in government budgets. Always check current HMRC guidance for anything that matters financially.
