Squad rules

How many players do I pick?

Exactly 5, from the pool of players across the 8 remaining teams.

Can I stack one team?

Max 2 players from the same team — stops everyone just drafting one favourite side.

Can I change my squad later?

Once a draft is submitted it's locked for the rest of the tournament — quarterfinals through the Final. No edits, no do-overs.
