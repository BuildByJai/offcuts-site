    Welcome to Book Town

    Every building here will hold a game built around a great book. The Study (north-west, the one with the columns) is open now — walk through its door to play.

    There are 50 coins dotted around town and 5 golden ones in sneaky spots. Roofs are fair game: stacks of crates by the café make a decent first step up, and the clock tower has handholds if you're brave.

    The other shops are opening soon — keep exploring!
