    Welcome to Book Town

    Every building here will hold a game built around a great book. The Study (north-west, the one with the columns) is open now — walk through its door to play.

    There are 63 coins dotted around town and 8 golden ones in sneaky spots. Roofs are fair game: stacks of crates by the café make a decent first step up, and the clock tower has handholds if you're brave.

    New: double jump, super jump (hold Charge), slide and ledge grab are yours from the start. The market stall by the billboard sells dash, wall jump, glide, ground pound and triple jump. Mushrooms, jump rings, ziplines, rails and a cannon are scattered round the square, and the chequered flags start three time trials.

    The other shops are opening soon — keep exploring!
