2025/26 tax bands

Basic rate (20%): £12,571 to £50,270

Higher rate (40%): £50,271 to £125,140

Between £100,000 and £125,140, the personal allowance is withdrawn at £1 for every £2 earned, creating an effective 60% marginal rate in that band.
