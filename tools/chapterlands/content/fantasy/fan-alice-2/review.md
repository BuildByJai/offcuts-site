# Review — Through the Looking-Glass

Right answer in **bold**. Evidence is the supporting sentence from the text (never shown to players).

## Looking-Glass House (Chapter 1) · PUZZLE

1. *what* — How did Alice get into the Looking-glass room?
   - **She went through the glass over the mantelpiece**
   - She opened a secret door
   - She fell down a rabbit-hole
   - Evidence (chapter 1): "In another moment Alice was through the glass, and had jumped lightly down into the Looking-glass room."
   - Hint: Look again at the part where the glass begins to melt away.

2. *why* — Why could Alice only read the poem by holding it up to a glass?
   - **It was a Looking-glass book, so the words went the wrong way**
   - The writing was too small
   - It was written in a secret code
   - Evidence (chapter 1): "And if I hold it up to a glass, the words will all go the right way again.”"
   - Hint: Look again at the part where Alice finds the book on the table.

3. *think* — What is different about the room behind the glass?
   - **It looks the same, but things go the other way**
   - Everything is much bigger
   - It is always night there
   - Evidence (chapter 1): "First, there’s the room you can see through the glass—that’s just the same as our drawing-room, only the things go the other way."
   - Hint: Look again at what Alice tells Kitty about Looking-glass House.
