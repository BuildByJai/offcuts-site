    Welcome to Chapterlands

    This is Library Square. Each of the seven roads leading out of it is one part of the library — Fantasy, Adventure, Animals & Nature, Friends & Family, Mystery, Sci-Fi and Life Skills. Every book is a district along its road: the easiest books sit nearest the square, the hardest at the far end.

    Reading opens doors. Read a chapter in the Reading Room (the building with the columns), answer three quick questions, and that chapter's street opens. Finish a whole book to earn its Key and a stamp on your library card.

    The roads are foggy. Climb the Lookout Tower on the Fantasy road to see what's coming. The Librarian has lost some books, and someone small and white is in a terrible hurry near the café…

    There are 63 coins dotted around town and 8 golden ones in sneaky spots. The market stall sells new moves.
