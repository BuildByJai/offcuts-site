How to play Sudoku

The grid is split into rows, columns and boxes. Every row, column and box must contain each symbol exactly once. Some cells start filled in. Use logic to work out the rest. You never need to guess, and you never need to do maths.

Tap a cell, then tap a symbol. Turn on Notes to pencil in candidates. Wrong entries turn red and count as a mistake.

Scoring

Every correct placement scores points, more on harder puzzles. Correct placements in a row build a combo multiplier (×2 after 4, up to ×5). A mistake or hint resets it. Finishing a row, column or box gives a bonus. At the end you get a time bonus, plus extras for zero mistakes and no hints. All your points also count as XP towards your level.

Other numeral systems

Sudoku is about logic, not arithmetic, so any set of symbols works. You can switch between Western digits, Eastern Arabic (١٢٣), Roman numerals, Chinese, Devanagari, letters, animals, fruit or colours at any time, even mid-game.
