How it works

Paste the full text of a real match report — a team news site, a live blog, your own notes — along with a short label for the match. An AI pass reads the report and pulls out a handful of talking-point moments with short scripts, grounding every detail (including the illustrative pitch shapes) in what the text actually describes. Each moment renders as an animated pitch clip you can narrate over or post with the built-in subtitles.

Why paste a report instead of picking a match?

An earlier version pulled fixtures automatically from a stats API. It technically worked, but categorical data (shot counts, cards, subs) has no descriptive texture — the AI had nothing to ground a scene in, so the pitch shapes came out looking random. A real match report already says who did what, where, which is what actually makes the shapes worth watching.

Are the player positions real?

No — they're an illustrative sketch grounded in what the report describes, not tracked positional data. Named, described players get deliberate placement; everyone else sits in a generic resting shape rather than being scattered at random. Use it as a storytelling aid, not a tactical record.

Moments and scripts generated by AI from a pasted match report — always verify before publishing. Not affiliated with the Premier League or any club.
