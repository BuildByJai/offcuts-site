How this works

When you click Start, the timer begins counting. Any activity — clicking, scrolling, typing, moving the mouse, or switching tabs — resets it. Beat your best time and it's saved automatically. The timer restarts right away so you can try again.

Common questions

Is my best time saved?

Yes, in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data will reset it.

Runs entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
