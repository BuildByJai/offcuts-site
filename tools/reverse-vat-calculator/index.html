If you have a price that already includes VAT and need to know the underlying net figure and the VAT portion, this tool does the reverse calculation for you.

How it works

Net = Gross ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). VAT = Gross − Net. This is the correct way to pull VAT back out of a total — you can't just take 20% off the gross, since that overstates the VAT.

Worked example

Gross: £120 at 20% VAT.

Net: £120 ÷ 1.20 = £100.00

VAT: £120 − £100 = £20.00

Common questions

Why can't I just take 20% off the total?

Taking 20% off £120 gives £96, which is wrong. The VAT was calculated on the net amount, not the gross, so you need to divide by 1.20 rather than multiply by 0.80.
