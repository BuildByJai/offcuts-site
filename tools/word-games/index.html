Word Gamesgames

7 word games for every age, from picture matching to speed spelling. Pick an age band, pick a game, play.

Every game asks you to pick a band before you start. Your choice is remembered for the rest of this visit — pick it once here, or in any game.

Common questions

Do I need to make an account?

No. Nothing is saved beyond your browser tab — no logins, no leaderboards, no data sent anywhere.

Which game should we start with?

Rhyme Time and Memory Match are the gentlest starting points for younger kids. Word Search and Word Builder suit older kids and adults who want more of a challenge.
